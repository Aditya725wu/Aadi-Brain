# Noise

## Definition

**Noise is unwanted electrical or electromagnetic energy that interferes with the transmitted signal.**

Noise can reduce the quality of communication.

## Types of Noise

### 1. Thermal Noise

Thermal noise is caused by the **random motion of electrons in a wire**, which creates an unwanted signal.

### 2. Induced Noise

Induced noise comes from sources such as:

- Motors
- Appliances
- Other electrical devices

These devices can act as transmitting antennas, while the medium can act as a receiving antenna.

### 3. Crosstalk

Crosstalk is unwanted interference **between two wires**.

The signal from one wire interferes with another wire.

### 4. Impulse Noise

Impulse noise consists of **spikes**.

It can result from:

- Power lines
- Lightning
- Sudden disturbances

## Comparison

| Noise Type | Main Cause |
|---|---|
| Thermal | Random electron motion |
| Induced | Motors/appliances/electrical sources |
| Crosstalk | Interference between wires |
| Impulse | Power lines, lightning, sudden spikes |

## Key Point

Noise does not simply mean that the original signal becomes weaker. It means an **unwanted component is added** to the communication signal.
