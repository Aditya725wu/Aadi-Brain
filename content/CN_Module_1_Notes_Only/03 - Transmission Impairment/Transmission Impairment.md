# Transmission Impairment

Transmission media are not perfect.

As a signal travels through a medium, the received signal may not be the same as the transmitted signal.

**What is sent is not always what is received.**

There are three major causes of transmission impairment:

1. **Attenuation**
2. **Distortion**
3. **Noise**

## 1. Attenuation

Signal loses energy and becomes weaker.

## 2. Distortion

The signal changes its form or shape.

## 3. Noise

Unwanted electrical or electromagnetic energy is added to the signal.

## Easy Memory

**Attenuation → Strength changes**

**Distortion → Shape changes**

**Noise → Unwanted signal added**
