# Distortion

## Definition

**Distortion means that the signal changes its form or shape.**

Distortion is particularly associated with **composite signals**.

## Why Does Distortion Occur?

A composite signal contains multiple frequency components.

Each frequency component can have its own propagation speed while travelling through a medium.

Therefore:

1. Different components travel differently.
2. They arrive at the receiver with different delays.
3. Their phases become different from their original phases.
4. The components no longer combine in exactly the same way.
5. The received composite signal changes shape.

## Simple Understanding

### At sender

Components are **in phase** and combine to form the original waveform.

### At receiver

Components become **out of phase** because of different delays.

The combined waveform therefore becomes distorted.

## Key Difference

**Attenuation changes signal strength.**

**Distortion changes signal shape.**
