# Wavelength and Propagation Speed

## Wavelength

**Wavelength (λ)** is the distance a signal travels during one complete cycle.

Unit: **metre (m)**.

It is related to propagation speed, frequency, and period.

> **FORMULA**
>
> `λ = vT`

Since `T = 1/f`:

> **FORMULA**
>
> `λ = v/f`

Therefore:

> **FORMULA**
>
> `v = fλ`

where:

- `λ` = wavelength
- `v` = propagation speed
- `T` = period
- `f` = frequency

## Propagation Speed

Propagation speed is the speed at which the signal/wave travels through the transmission medium.

Unit: **m/s**.

It is different from **bit rate**.

- Propagation speed → m/s
- Bit rate → bit/s

## Numerical Example

Given:

`v = 2 × 10^8 m/s`

`f = 1 MHz = 10^6 Hz`

Using:

`λ = v/f`

`λ = (2 × 10^8)/(10^6)`

`λ = 200 m`

Therefore, wavelength = **200 m**.
