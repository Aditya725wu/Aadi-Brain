# Digital Signals

A digital signal has a limited number of signal levels.

## Signal Level

A signal level is a particular amplitude value used to represent data.

A digital signal can use:

- 2 levels
- 4 levels
- 8 levels
- etc.

## Digital Signal as Composite Signal

A digital signal can be viewed as a **composite analog signal with infinite bandwidth**.

## Periodic and Nonperiodic Digital Signals

Digital signals may be periodic or nonperiodic.

A repeating digital pattern is periodic.

A non-repeating pattern is nonperiodic.

## Baseband

Baseband transmission sends the digital signal directly through a low-pass channel.

## Bandpass

If the available channel is bandpass, the digital signal cannot be sent directly. It must first be converted into an analog signal.

## Important Point

A low-pass channel can carry a baseband digital signal if it has an infinite or sufficiently wide bandwidth to preserve the signal shape.
