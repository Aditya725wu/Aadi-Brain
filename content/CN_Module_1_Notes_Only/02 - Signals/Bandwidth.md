# Bandwidth

## Definition

Bandwidth is the **range of frequencies contained in a signal**.

For a signal with a lowest frequency `f_L` and highest frequency `f_H`:

> **FORMULA**
>
> `B = f_H - f_L`

where:

- `B` = bandwidth
- `f_H` = highest frequency
- `f_L` = lowest frequency

Unit: **Hz**

## Example

A signal contains frequencies:

`100 Hz, 300 Hz, 500 Hz, 700 Hz, 900 Hz`

Lowest frequency:

`f_L = 100 Hz`

Highest frequency:

`f_H = 900 Hz`

Therefore:

`B = 900 - 100`

`B = 800 Hz`

## Important

Bandwidth is **not the highest frequency itself**.

It is the **difference between highest and lowest frequencies**.

## Numerical Example

If:

`f_L = 2 kHz`

`f_H = 8 kHz`

Then:

`B = 8 - 2 = 6 kHz`

Therefore, bandwidth = **6 kHz**.
