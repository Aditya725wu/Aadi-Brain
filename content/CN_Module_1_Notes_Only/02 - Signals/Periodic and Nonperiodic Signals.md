# Periodic and Nonperiodic Signals

## Periodic Signal

A periodic signal completes a pattern that **repeats after a fixed interval of time**.

That fixed interval is the **period (T)**.

### Formula

> **FORMULA**
>
> `f = 1/T`

A periodic analog signal can be:

1. **Simple** — one sine wave
2. **Composite** — multiple sine waves

## Nonperiodic Signal

A nonperiodic signal does **not repeat the same pattern at regular intervals**.

In data communications, periodic analog signals and nonperiodic digital signals are commonly used.

## Period vs Frequency

- Small period → more cycles per second → high frequency
- Large period → fewer cycles per second → low frequency

### Numerical Example

If `T = 0.25 s`:

`f = 1/T`

`f = 1/0.25 = 4 Hz`

Therefore, the signal has a frequency of **4 Hz**.

If `f = 50 Hz`:

`T = 1/f = 1/50 = 0.02 s = 20 ms`

## Remember

**Frequency and period are inverses of each other.**
