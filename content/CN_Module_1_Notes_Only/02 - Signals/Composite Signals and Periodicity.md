# Composite Signals and Periodicity

## Periodic Composite Signal

If the composite signal is **periodic**, its decomposition gives a series of signals with **discrete frequencies**.

Example:

`f, 3f, 9f, ...`

These appear as separate spikes in the frequency domain.

## Nonperiodic Composite Signal

If the composite signal is **nonperiodic**, its decomposition gives a combination of sine waves with **continuous frequencies**.

Therefore, the frequency domain is a continuous range rather than isolated frequency spikes.

## Comparison

| Composite Signal | Frequency Components |
|---|---|
| Periodic | Discrete frequencies |
| Nonperiodic | Continuous frequencies |

## Key Point

**Periodic → discrete frequencies**

**Nonperiodic → continuous frequencies**
