# Important Formula Sheet

> **FORMULA — Frequency**
>
> `f = 1/T`

> **FORMULA — Period**
>
> `T = 1/f`

> **FORMULA — Wavelength**
>
> `λ = v/f = vT`

> **FORMULA — Bandwidth**
>
> `B = f_H - f_L`

> **FORMULA — Bit Length**
>
> `Bit length = propagation speed × bit duration`

> **FORMULA — dB**
>
> `dB = 10 log10(P2/P1)`

> **FORMULA — SNR**
>
> `SNR = P_signal / P_noise`

> **FORMULA — SNR in dB**
>
> `SNR_dB = 10 log10(SNR)`

> **FORMULA — Nyquist**
>
> `Bit rate = 2B log2(L)`

> **FORMULA — Shannon**
>
> `C = B log2(1 + SNR)`

# Numerical Units to Remember

- Frequency → Hz, kHz, MHz, GHz
- Period → second, millisecond, microsecond
- Wavelength → metre
- Propagation speed → m/s
- Bandwidth → Hz
- Bit rate → bit/s, kbps, Mbps
- SNR → ratio; SNRdB → dB
- Power → W, mW, μW
