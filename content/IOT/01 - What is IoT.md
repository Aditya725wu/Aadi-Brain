# What is IoT?

## Definition

IoT = Internet of Things.

IoT is a technology in which physical devices are connected to a network or the internet so they can collect, communicate, process, and sometimes act on data.

## Simple Example

Smart Water Pump:

User
↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

## Interview Answer

> IoT stands for Internet of Things. It refers to connecting physical devices to a network or the internet so they can collect, communicate, process, and sometimes act on data.

## Remember

**IoT = Physical Device + Connectivity + Data + Action**
