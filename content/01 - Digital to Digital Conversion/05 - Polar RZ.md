# Polar RZ

## Definition
RZ = Return-to-Zero.

The signal returns to `0V` during the bit interval.

## NRZ vs RZ
- **NRZ:** stays at its level for the whole bit.
- **RZ:** returns to zero within the bit.

## Limitation
Polar RZ is more complex and requires greater bandwidth than NRZ.

## Memory
**RZ → returns to zero**
