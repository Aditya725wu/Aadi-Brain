# IKS Lecture 05 — Indian Knowledge Systems

> [!info] Lecture Overview  
> This lecture discusses the development of Indian knowledge and technology, Harappan civilization, Vedic knowledge, Vedangas, Upavedas, agriculture, water management, mining, transportation, architecture, and the relevance of traditional knowledge to modern society.

---

## 1. Technology and Human Welfare

### Main Idea

The lecture begins with the idea that **technology should ultimately serve human welfare**.

Technology should not be developed only for:

- Money
    
- Market
    
- Competition
    
- Power
    

Instead, technological development should consider:

- Human welfare
    
- Society
    
- Employment
    
- Environment
    
- Quality of life
    

> **Key Idea:** Technology → Human Welfare

# 17. Ancient Dentistry — Mehrgarh

### Mehrgarh — मेहरगड

**Pronunciation:** मे-हर-गड

The lecture refers to a **2006 Nature paper**.

Researchers found:

- 11 drilled molar crowns
    
- From 9 adults
    
- At a Neolithic graveyard at Mehrgarh
    

This was interpreted as evidence of **proto-dentistry** in an early farming culture.
