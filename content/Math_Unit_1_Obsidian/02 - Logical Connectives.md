# Logical Connectives

| Connective | Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| NOT | `¬p` | Negation |
| AND | `p ∧ q` | Conjunction |
| OR | `p ∨ q` | Disjunction |
| Implication | `p → q` | If p then q |
| Biconditional | `p ↔ q` | p if and only if q |

## NOT

| p | ¬p |
|---|---|
| T | F |
| F | T |

## AND

`p ∧ q` is true only when both p and q are true.

| p | q | p ∧ q |
|---|---|---|
| T | T | T |
| T | F | F |
| F | T | F |
| F | F | F |

## OR

`p ∨ q` is false only when both p and q are false.

| p | q | p ∨ q |
|---|---|---|
| T | T | T |
| T | F | T |
| F | T | T |
| F | F | F |
