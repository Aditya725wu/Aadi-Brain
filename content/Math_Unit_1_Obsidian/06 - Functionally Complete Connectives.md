# Functionally Complete Set of Connectives

A set of connectives is **functionally complete** if every logical formula can be expressed using connectives from that set.

## NAND

`p ↑ q = ¬(p ∧ q)`

| p | q | p ↑ q |
|---|---|---|
| T | T | F |
| T | F | T |
| F | T | T |
| F | F | T |

## NOR

`p ↓ q = ¬(p ∨ q)`

| p | q | p ↓ q |
|---|---|---|
| T | T | F |
| T | F | F |
| F | T | F |
| F | F | T |

## Important

The unit notes treat NAND and NOR as functionally complete systems.
