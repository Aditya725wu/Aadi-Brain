# Statements and Propositions

## Statement

A statement is a declarative sentence having exactly one truth value: **True** or **False**.

## Atomic Statement

A statement that cannot be broken into smaller statements.

Example:

`p = Today is Monday.`

## Compound Statement

A statement formed by combining two or more atomic statements using logical connectives.

Example:

`p ∧ q`

## Important

A statement must have a definite truth value.
