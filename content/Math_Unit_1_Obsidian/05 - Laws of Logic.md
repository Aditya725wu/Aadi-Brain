# Laws of Logic

## Identity

`p ∧ T ≡ p`

`p ∨ F ≡ p`

## Domination

`p ∨ T ≡ T`

`p ∧ F ≡ F`

## Double Negation

`¬(¬p) ≡ p`

## Idempotent

`p ∨ p ≡ p`

`p ∧ p ≡ p`

## Commutative

`p ∨ q ≡ q ∨ p`

`p ∧ q ≡ q ∧ p`

## Associative

`(p ∨ q) ∨ r ≡ p ∨ (q ∨ r)`

`(p ∧ q) ∧ r ≡ p ∧ (q ∧ r)`

## Distributive

`p ∨ (q ∧ r) ≡ (p ∨ q) ∧ (p ∨ r)`

`p ∧ (q ∨ r) ≡ (p ∧ q) ∨ (p ∧ r)`

## De Morgan's Laws

`¬(p ∨ q) ≡ ¬p ∧ ¬q`

`¬(p ∧ q) ≡ ¬p ∨ ¬q`

## Absorption

`p ∨ (p ∧ q) ≡ p`

`p ∧ (p ∨ q) ≡ p`

## Negation

`p ∨ ¬p ≡ T`

`p ∧ ¬p ≡ F`

## Exam Use

Use these laws to simplify logical expressions and prove logical equivalence.
