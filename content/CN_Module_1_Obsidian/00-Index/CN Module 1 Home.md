# 🧮 Important Formulas

### Frequency and Period

$$
f = \frac{1}{T}
$$

$$
T = \frac{1}{f}
$$

### Wavelength

$$
\lambda = \frac{v}{f}
$$

### Bandwidth

$$
B = f_H - f_L
$$

### Decibel

$$
dB = 10\log_{10}\left(\frac{P_2}{P_1}\right)
$$

### SNR

$$
SNR = \frac{P_s}{P_n}
$$

$$
SNR_{dB}=10\log_{10}(SNR)
$$

### Nyquist

$$
Bit\ Rate = 2B\log_2 L
$$

### Shannon

$$
C = B\log_2(1+SNR)
$$

### Bits per Signal Level

$$
bits/level = \log_2 L
$$
