# 4-Mark Answer Template — Transmission Impairment

## Definition
Transmission impairment is any effect that causes the received signal to differ from the transmitted signal.

## Classification
1. **Attenuation:** signal loses energy and becomes weaker.
2. **Distortion:** signal shape changes; in a composite signal, different frequency components can experience different propagation speeds/delays and phase changes.
3. **Noise:** unwanted signal/disturbance is added to the original signal.

## Diagram
Draw: Sender → channel → Receiver, and show weaker / changed / noisy cases.

## Conclusion
Therefore, attenuation affects strength, distortion affects shape, and noise adds unwanted disturbance.
