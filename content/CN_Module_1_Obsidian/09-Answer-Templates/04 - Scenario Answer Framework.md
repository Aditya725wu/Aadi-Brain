# Scenario Answer Framework

When the question begins with “A network/system/channel…” do not jump directly to a definition.

### Step 1 — Extract clues
Underline symptoms, numbers, devices, protocols and constraints.

### Step 2 — Identify the concept
Write: “The concept involved is ___ because ___.”

### Step 3 — Explain mechanism
Give the cause → process → result chain.

### Step 4 — Diagram
Use a small labelled diagram whenever the concept has a flow/architecture.

### Step 5 — Apply
Explicitly connect the theory to the given scenario.

### Step 6 — Conclude
One sentence answering exactly what the question asked.
