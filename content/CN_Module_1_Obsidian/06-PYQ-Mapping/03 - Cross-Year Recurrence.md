# 🔁 Cross-Year CN Pattern Analysis

## 2025 vs 2026

| Skill tested | 2025 paper | 2026 paper | Preparation implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scenario/application | Yes | Yes | Practice identifying concepts from situations |
| Numerical/problem solving | Strong | Present in waveform construction | Do not skip calculations |
| Protocol working | ARQ/routing | OSI encapsulation | Learn step-by-step flows |
| Diagram/format | IPv6/header | OSI mapping/waveforms | Practice labelled diagrams |
| Comparison | Protocol/routing concepts | OSI vs TCP/IP, FDM/TDM/WDM | Build comparison tables |
| Real-world context | Routing/network operation | Web page, Ethernet/communication | Expect contextual wording |

## Strong recurring exam behavior
The papers repeatedly turn a syllabus concept into an **operation or scenario**.

Examples:
- “A student opens a web page…” → OSI encapsulation.
- “A digital signal becomes weaker…” → transmission impairment.
- “For a binary sequence…” → line-code waveform.
- “A computer uses a sliding window…” → ARQ calculation.
- “A destination address and mask are given…” → routing/subnet reasoning.

## Preparation rule
For every theory topic, prepare four forms:
1. **Definition**
2. **Diagram**
3. **Application scenario**
4. **Numerical/comparison if applicable**
