# 2026 CN MSE — Question Map

## Paper evidence
The uploaded 2026 WCE CN MSE paper (04/09/2026, Computer Network 7CS204, 30 marks) asks:

### Q1A — 4 marks
A student opens a web page from a remote server. Explain **encapsulation at the sender and decapsulation at the receiver**, and state the role of **Network and Transport layers**.

**Type:** Application/scenario + layered explanation.

**Topics:** [[02-Topics/25 - OSI Reference Model]], [[02-Topics/27 - OSI vs TCP-IP]]

### Q1B — 4 marks
Compare **OSI reference model and TCP/IP protocol suite** with respect to layer structure, functions and practical use; show mapping.

**Type:** Comparison + diagram/mapping.

### Q1C — 2 marks
A digital signal becomes weaker, its waveform changes, and unwanted electrical disturbances appear. Identify the three transmission impairments and explain briefly.

**Type:** Scenario identification.

**Topics:** [[02-Topics/16 - Transmission Impairment]], [[02-Topics/17 - Attenuation]], [[02-Topics/18 - Distortion]], [[02-Topics/19 - Noise]]

### Q2A — 4 marks
For binary sequence `110010`, draw waveforms for **Unipolar NRZ, Polar NRZ, Bipolar AMI and Manchester**, marking bit boundaries and transitions.

**Type:** Application/diagram construction.

### Q2B — 6 marks
Explain and compare **FDM, TDM and WDM**, with basic working principle and one suitable application/advantage.

**Type:** Long theory + comparison + application.

## Pattern extracted
The paper combines:
- scenario-based theory
- direct comparison
- identification from symptoms
- waveform drawing
- long comparison with applications

This means preparation should not stop at definitions.
