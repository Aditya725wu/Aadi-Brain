# Sample Question --- Using Both Limits

**Q. A channel has bandwidth 1 MHz and SNR 63. Find the Shannon upper
limit. If a practical rate of 4 Mbps is chosen, use Nyquist to determine
the required number of levels.**

### Answer plan

1.  Shannon first.
2.  State upper limit.
3.  Choose practical rate 4 Mbps.
4.  Apply Nyquist.
5.  Solve for L.
