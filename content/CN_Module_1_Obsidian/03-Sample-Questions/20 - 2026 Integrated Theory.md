# Sample Question --- Integrated 2026 Pattern

**Q. A digital signal travelling through a communication link becomes
weaker, its waveform changes, and unwanted electrical disturbances are
observed at the receiver. Identify the three transmission impairments
and explain each briefly.**

### Expected 2-mark style

1.  Attenuation --- loss of energy, weaker signal.
2.  Distortion --- signal form/shape changes.
3.  Noise --- unwanted electrical signal/disturbance.
