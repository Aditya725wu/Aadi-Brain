# 🎯 What WCE Actually Asks in CN

## Core observation
The uploaded papers show that WCE can phrase a familiar concept as a real network situation.

### Example: OSI
Instead of only asking “Explain OSI,” the 2026 paper uses a web-page scenario and asks for encapsulation/decapsulation plus Network and Transport layer roles.

### Example: Transmission impairment
Instead of separately defining attenuation, distortion and noise, the paper describes three symptoms and asks the student to identify them.

### Example: Line coding
Instead of asking only “Define NRZ,” the paper gives a binary sequence and requires actual waveform construction.

### Example: Multiplexing
The paper asks FDM/TDM/WDM as a comparison plus an application/advantage.

## Therefore every topic needs
- **What is it?**
- **How does it work?**
- **Where is it used?**
- **How do I identify it from a scenario?**
- **Can I draw it?**
- **Can I calculate something from it?**
- **What can it be compared with?**
