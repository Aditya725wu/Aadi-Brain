# 📊 Question Pattern Analysis — 2025 vs 2026

## Pattern 1 — Application framing
Questions are frequently written as a situation rather than a chapter heading.

**Preparation:** Convert every definition into a “given situation → identify/explain/justify” question.

## Pattern 2 — Diagram is not decoration
OSI mapping, IPv6 header, line coding and similar questions require labelled structures. A diagram should be part of the answer, not an afterthought.

## Pattern 3 — Compare + apply
2026 explicitly asks FDM/TDM/WDM with an application or advantage. Comparison tables alone are insufficient; know why a technique is used.

## Pattern 4 — Numerical reasoning
The 2025 CN paper includes subnetting, ARQ and timing calculations. For any topic containing a formula, prepare at least three variations.

## Pattern 5 — Integrated questions
The 2026 web-page question combines scenario + OSI + encapsulation + Network + Transport layers.

## Exam strategy
If you can answer only definitions, you are underprepared. Your minimum target is:

**Definition + diagram + scenario + one problem.**
