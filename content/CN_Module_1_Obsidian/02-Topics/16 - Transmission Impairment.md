# Transmission Impairment

## Definition

Transmission media are not perfect. Because of these imperfections, the
signal at the end of the medium may differ from the signal at the
beginning.

The PPT identifies three causes: 1. Attenuation 2. Distortion 3. Noise

## Memory

  Impairment    What happens?
  ------------- --------------------------------------
  Attenuation   Signal loses energy / becomes weaker
  Distortion    Signal changes form/shape
  Noise         Unwanted extra signal

## Sample

**Q. A digital signal becomes weaker, its waveform changes, and unwanted
electrical disturbances appear at the receiver. Identify the three
transmission impairments and explain them.**
