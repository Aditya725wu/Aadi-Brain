# Nyquist Bit Rate

## Use

Used for a **noiseless channel**.

## Meaning

Theoretical maximum bit rate of a noiseless channel.

## Formula

\[ `\boxed{Bit\ Rate=2B\log_2L}`{=tex} \]

where: - (B) = bandwidth in Hz - (L) = number of signal levels

## Example

If: \[ B=3000Hz,`\quad `{=tex}L=4 \]

\[ Bit Rate=2(3000)`\log`{=tex}\_2 4 \]

\[ =6000(2)=12000bps \]
