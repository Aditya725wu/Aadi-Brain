# Digital Signals

## Definition

Information can be represented using a digital signal.

Example: - 1 can be represented by a positive voltage. - 0 can be
represented by zero voltage.

A digital signal can also have more than two levels.

## Key idea

More levels can allow more than one bit to be represented by one signal
element.
