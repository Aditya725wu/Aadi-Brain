# Bandwidth

## Definition

The bandwidth of a composite signal is the difference between its
highest and lowest frequencies.

\[ `\boxed{B=f_H-f_L}`{=tex} \]

## Example

Frequencies: 100, 300, 500, 700, 900 Hz

\[ f_H=900Hz,`\quad `{=tex}f_L=100Hz \]

\[ B=900-100=800Hz \]

## Important

Bandwidth is a **frequency range**, not simply the highest frequency.

## Sample

**Q. A signal contains frequencies 100, 300, 500, 700 and 900 Hz. Find
its bandwidth and draw its spectrum.**
