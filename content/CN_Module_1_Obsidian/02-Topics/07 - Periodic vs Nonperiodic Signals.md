# Periodic vs Nonperiodic Signals

## Periodic signal

A signal pattern repeats at regular intervals.

## Nonperiodic signal

The pattern does not repeat regularly.

## In data communications

The supplied PPT notes that periodic analog signals and nonperiodic
digital signals are commonly used.

## Frequency-domain relation

-   Periodic composite signal → discrete frequency components.
-   Nonperiodic composite signal → continuous frequency components.
