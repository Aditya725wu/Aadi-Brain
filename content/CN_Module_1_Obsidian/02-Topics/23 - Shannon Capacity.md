# Shannon Capacity

## Use

Used for a **noisy channel**.

## Meaning

Theoretical data-rate capacity of a noisy channel.

## Formula

\[ `\boxed{C=B\log_2(1+SNR)}`{=tex} \]

where: - (C) = channel capacity in bps - (B) = bandwidth in Hz - SNR =
signal-to-noise ratio

## Important

If SNR approaches zero, capacity approaches zero regardless of
bandwidth.

## Example pattern

Given (B) and SNR: 1. Put values in formula. 2. Calculate (1+SNR). 3.
Take (`\log`{=tex}\_2). 4. Multiply by (B). 5. Give answer in
bps/kbps/Mbps.

## Sample

**Q. A channel has bandwidth 3000 Hz and SNR 3162. Find Shannon
capacity.**
