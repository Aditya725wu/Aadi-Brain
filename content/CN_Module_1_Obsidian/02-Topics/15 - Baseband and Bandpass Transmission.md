# Baseband and Bandpass Transmission

## Baseband

A digital signal is transmitted directly over a low-pass channel.

Preserving the shape of a digital signal ideally requires a very
wide/infinite-bandwidth low-pass channel.

## Bandpass

If the available channel is bandpass, the digital signal cannot be sent
directly in its original form; it is converted to an analog signal
before transmission.

## Exam comparison

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
