# Data Rate Limits

## Definition

Data rate is how fast data can be sent over a channel, measured in bits
per second.

## Three factors in the PPT

1.  Available bandwidth
2.  Signal levels used
3.  Channel quality / level of noise

## Two theoretical limits

-   Noiseless channel → \[\[22 - Nyquist Bit Rate\]\]
-   Noisy channel → \[\[23 - Shannon Capacity\]\]

## Important observation

Increasing signal levels can increase possible bit rate, but may reduce
reliability.
