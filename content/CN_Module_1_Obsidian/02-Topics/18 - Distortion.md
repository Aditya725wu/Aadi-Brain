# Distortion

## Definition

Distortion means that the signal changes its form or shape.

## Why in composite signals?

A composite signal contains multiple frequency components.

Each component can have its own propagation speed through a medium.

Therefore the components can arrive with different delays.

Different delays cause different phase relationships at the receiver.

When the components combine again, the resulting waveform can have a
different shape.

## Chain to memorize

``` text
Different propagation speeds
        ↓
Different delays
        ↓
Different phases
        ↓
Changed waveform shape
        ↓
Distortion
```
