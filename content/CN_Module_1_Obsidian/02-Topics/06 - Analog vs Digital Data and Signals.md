# Analog vs Digital Data and Signals

## Analog data

Continuous and takes continuous values.

## Digital data

Has discrete states and takes discrete values.

## Analog signal

Can have an infinite number of values in a range.

## Digital signal

Has a limited number of values.

## Comparison

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Basis                   Analog                  Digital
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  Values                  Continuous/infinite in  Limited/discrete
                          range                   

  Shape                   Smooth                  Discrete levels

  Example idea            Sine-like continuous    0/1 or multiple levels
                          waveform                
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
