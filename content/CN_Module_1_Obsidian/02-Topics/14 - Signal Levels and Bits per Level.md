# Signal Levels and Bits per Level

## Core relation

If a digital signal has (L) levels:

\[ `\boxed{n=\log_2L}`{=tex} \]

where (n) is bits represented per signal element.

## Examples

2 levels: \[ `\log`{=tex}\_2 2=1 \]

4 levels: \[ `\log`{=tex}\_2 4=2 \]

8 levels: \[ `\log`{=tex}\_2 8=3 \]

16 levels: \[ `\log`{=tex}\_2 16=4 \]

## Important limitation

Increasing signal levels can reduce system reliability because levels
become harder to distinguish in the presence of noise.

## Sample

**Q. A digital signal has 8 levels. How many bits are needed per signal
level?**

Answer: \[ n=`\log`{=tex}\_2 8=3`\text{ bits}`{=tex} \]
