# Sine Wave Parameters

A sine wave is described by:

-   Amplitude
-   Frequency
-   Period
-   Phase
-   Wavelength

## Amplitude

Maximum strength/height of the signal.

## Frequency (f)

Rate of change with respect to time; measured in hertz.

High frequency → more cycles in the same time. Low frequency → fewer
cycles.

## Period (T)

Time required for one complete cycle.

\[ f=1/T \]

\[ T=1/f \]

## Phase

Position of the waveform relative to time 0.

## Wavelength (λ)

Physical distance a signal travels during one period.

\[ `\lambda`{=tex}=vT=v/f \]

where (v) is propagation speed.
