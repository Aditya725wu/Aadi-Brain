# Noise

## Definition

Noise is an unwanted electrical signal/disturbance that affects the
original signal during transmission.

## Types

### 1. Thermal noise

Random movement of electrons in a wire creates an extra signal.

### 2. Induced noise

Comes from motors and appliances; devices can act as transmitter
antennas and the medium as a receiving antenna.

### 3. Crosstalk

Similar interference, but between two wires.

### 4. Impulse noise

Sudden spikes caused by sources such as power lines and lightning.

## Memory

**Thermal → electrons** **Induced → devices** **Crosstalk → two wires**
**Impulse → spikes**
