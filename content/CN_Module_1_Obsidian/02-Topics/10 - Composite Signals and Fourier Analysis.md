# Composite Signals and Fourier Analysis

## Composite signal

A composite signal is made of many simple sine waves.

A single-frequency sine wave is not useful for typical data
communication; information is represented using composite signals.

## Fourier analysis

According to the PPT, a composite signal can be viewed as a combination
of simple sine waves with different: - frequencies, - amplitudes, -
phases.

## Periodicity

### Periodic composite

Decomposition gives discrete frequencies.

### Nonperiodic composite

Decomposition gives continuous frequencies.

## Example

A composite signal may contain: \[ f,;3f,;9f \]

Frequency-domain representation shows spikes at those frequencies. Spike
position represents frequency; spike height represents amplitude.
