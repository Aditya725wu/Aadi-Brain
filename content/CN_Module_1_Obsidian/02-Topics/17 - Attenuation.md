# Attenuation

## Definition

Attenuation means loss of energy, resulting in a weaker signal.

When a signal travels through a medium, it loses energy while overcoming
resistance.

## Compensation

Amplifiers can be used to compensate for the energy loss.

## Measurement

Decibel (dB) is used to express loss or gain of energy.

\[ `\boxed{dB=10\log_{10}(P_2/P_1)}`{=tex} \]

-   (P_1) = input signal power
-   (P_2) = output signal power

## Important example

If: \[ P_2=`\frac`{=tex}12P_1 \]

then: \[ dB`\approx`{=tex}-3dB \]

A loss of 3 dB corresponds to half the power.
